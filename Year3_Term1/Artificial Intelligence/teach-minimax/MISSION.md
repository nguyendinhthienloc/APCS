# Mission: Minimax (CS420 Artificial Intelligence)

## Why
Pass the upcoming CS420 exam on adversarial search (Lecture 2-P1): score full marks on the two expected question types.

## Success looks like
- **Q1:** Fill in minimax values for every node of a 2–3 ply tree, and write out the α-β steps (α, β, v at each node, which branches are pruned) without errors
- **Q2.1:** Write a proof of α-β's efficiency: why it returns the same value as minimax, and why perfect ordering examines only O(b^(m/2)) nodes
- **Q2.2:** Answer true/false statements comparing Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS) with α-β, each with a one-line justification
- Also know expectimax and the slide-level theory (complexity, cutoff, horizon effect, quiescence) in case they come up

## Constraints
- Exam-driven: lecture slides (2025-CS420-Lecture2-P1-Minimax.pdf) and AIMA 4e Ch. 5 (§5.2–5.4) define scope and notation
- Short lessons, heavy on hand-tracing and exam-style practice
- Prefers a concrete story before the formal rule (see learning record 0001)

## Out of scope
- Implementing game engines; AlphaGo internals beyond the slide-level overview
