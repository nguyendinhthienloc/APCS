# Minimax intuition clicked via the box/envelope game, not via the rule

The bare rule (▲ = max, ▼ = min) did not land; the student asked "what is it for". A 2-box/2-envelope money story (you pick a box, opponent picks your envelope) made it click: they correctly chose Box R ($5, $100) over L ($3, $12) because R guarantees $5. Lead new topics (α-β, expectimax) with a concrete story before the rule.

**Evidence**: Answered the R = {$5, $100} check correctly, but phrased it "I pick $100", so they still blur "choosing a box" with "getting an envelope". Watch for that: the value of a node is what you are *guaranteed*, not the best leaf. They also confused "absolute min" with role-swapping earlier.
